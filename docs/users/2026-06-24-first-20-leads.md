# First 20 Design-Partner Lead List

Date: 2026-06-24

Status: Research artifact. No outreach recorded.

Purpose: identify the first 20 company/team-level accounts to research for
InstantML private-beta discovery. This is not a private contact database and
should not be treated as evidence that any team has agreed to talk. Contact
details below are limited to public company routes, official role-based inboxes,
public founder/executive profiles, or individual org-domain emails published by
the person or organization. Do not add guessed emails or data-broker contacts.

## Selection Criteria

The first lead list prioritizes accounts with public evidence of at least one
of these signals:

- Current or recent use of W&B, Comet, Neptune, MLflow, TensorBoard, or custom
  tracking.
- Training-loop or model-development workflows with many experiments, metrics,
  artifacts, checkpoints, or model versions.
- Small-to-mid-size commercial AI teams where a founder-led private beta can
  reach the buyer/user without enterprise procurement.
- Public pain adjacent to InstantML's wedge: comparison, velocity, cost,
  reproducibility, data/control, migration, artifacts, or operational overhead.

Fit score:

- `9-10`: strong first-wave candidate; clear current tracker and direct wedge.
- `7-8`: useful discovery or second-wave candidate; strong workflow but some
  buying or scope risk.
- `5-6`: keep warm; too large, too enterprise, too happy with incumbent, or
  stronger fit for later platform features.

## Recommended First Five

Start with these because they combine real tracker use, many-run workflow
evidence, and a plausible private-beta conversation.

1. **Captur**: edge/mobile computer vision, W&B run comparison, artifacts,
   GPU/system metrics, and model lifecycle evidence.
2. **Rime**: small speech/TTS team, W&B as experiment source of truth, audio
   artifacts, checkpoints, custom views, and long training cadence.
3. **CareRev**: Comet customer that explicitly moved from MLflow after scaling
   pain; strong positioning conversation around hosted UI and lower-friction
   experiment comparison.
4. **Pento.ai**: ML consulting/computer-vision team using Comet panels and
   custom visualization; good research partner for reusable dashboards and CV
   evidence workflows.
5. **Bioptimus**: Neptune/foundation-model tracking signal is exceptionally
   strong. Only approach after the beta limitations and durability stance are
   explicit, because data sensitivity and trust bar will be high.

- **Gretel**: excellent W&B evidence, including 50-100 experiments per compute
  block and synthetic-data training loops, but public reporting says NVIDIA
  acquired Gretel in 2025. Keep as a reference account, not first outbound.
  Sources: [W&B Gretel story](https://wandb.ai/site/customers-new/gretel/),
  [CRN acquisition report](https://www.crn.com/news/ai/2025/nvidia-buys-synthetic-data-startup-gretel-to-boost-ai-llms-report).
- **Cohere, Siemens, Toyota Research Institute, IBM, Pinterest, Canva, Square,
  Salesforce, Microsoft, Netflix, Uber, NatWest**: strong evidence of tracker
  use but too enterprise or procurement-heavy for the first private-beta wedge.
- **Trainy/Pluto, Minfx, Lightning AI, GoodSeed, ZenML**: important Neptune
  migration competitors or ecosystem players rather than first customer leads.
- **Unsloth**: strong adjacent fine-tuning ecosystem and W&B friction appears
  in public issues, but Unsloth is more partner/competitor/community channel
  than a clean design-partner account. Keep for integration/partnership
  research.

## Suggested Message Angles

Captur:

```text
I saw your W&B story around edge CV model releases, GPU metrics, artifacts, and
comparing runs. InstantML is testing a narrower hosted tracker for small ML
teams that want faster many-run comparison and exportable experiment history.
Would you be open to a 20-minute workflow interview on where W&B works well and
where comparison/artifact workflows still create drag?
```

Rime:

```text
Your W&B story is almost exactly the workflow InstantML is trying to serve:
small speech-model team, audio artifacts, long training cadences, checkpoints,
and custom views. I am looking for design partners willing to shadow-log or
import one non-critical project and tell us where the workflow is better or
worse than W&B.
```

CareRev:

```text
I noticed your Comet story mentioned starting with MLflow and then needing a
more complete hosted experiment workflow as the team scaled. InstantML is
testing that exact wedge: hosted many-run comparison and artifacts, without
asking teams to operate tracking infra. Could I ask what made MLflow fall short
and what would make a new tracker worth piloting?
```

Pento.ai:

```text
Pento's Comet Panels story makes you a great evaluator for InstantML's run
workspace direction. We are not trying to replace every Comet feature; we are
testing whether fast hosted run comparison plus exportable artifacts is valuable
for small expert ML teams. Could I ask a few questions about your CV experiment
review workflow?
```

Bioptimus:

```text
I saw your Neptune story about foundation-model training, hardware metrics,
dependencies, and real-time debugging. InstantML has a local-first Neptune
Exporter import path, and I am trying to learn what exported Neptune users need
before trusting a new tracker. Would you be open to a discovery conversation?
```

## Notes For Outreach Hygiene

- Do not imply any competitor failed them. These public stories are mostly
  positive incumbent stories; use them as evidence of workflow fit, not churn
  intent.
- Do not mention private data, internal costs, or procurement assumptions.
- Ask for workflow feedback first. Offer a pilot only after confirming pain.
- Use company contact forms, warm intros, or public professional profiles.
  Avoid storing private personal emails in this repository.
- If a company is a public W&B/Comet success story, assume they may be happy
  with the incumbent. The wedge is "can we learn from your workflow and safely
  shadow/import one project," not "switch now."
